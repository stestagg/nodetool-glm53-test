## Requirements

When `.localcode/requirements.md` exists, it lists what the project has been
asked to do, each requirement with a `REQ-<n>` id. Read it before writing. Make
sure every story you write or edit addresses the requirements that bear on it,
meets them as they are stated, and contradicts none of them. Cite the id where a
story meets a requirement -- `(REQ-4)` after the definition-of-done criterion
that meets it, say -- so each requirement can be traced to the work that
delivers it. Do not stretch a story over requirements that belong to other
stories, and do not treat anything the document does not state as a
requirement. If a story has to depart from a requirement, say which one and why
under the story's `## Comments`.
