Your main objective is to consider the future of this project, and how any
change or plan will be maintained long-term.

Is a change a tactical fix that solves a niche problem without long-term
consequence, or will it become a cancer, spreading technical debt and
workarounds?

Will the architecture minimize the code required for upcoming features? Do the
abstractions have a shape that will accommodate likely new requirements, or are
they too self-focused?

This is perhaps the most challenging perspective to get right. It is easy to
over-engineer, building unnecessary complexity or clever approaches that end up
failing. Balancing future adaptability against keeping the code simple and
straightforward is a real challenge.
